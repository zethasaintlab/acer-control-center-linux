# Visual Quality Invariant

Reject generic AI-dashboard patterns. Avoid meaningless gradients, excessive glass/glow, nested cards, arbitrary pills/badges, emoji icons, fake telemetry, inconsistent spacing, and purposeless motion. Design information hierarchy and interaction states before decoration. Use a coherent typography, spacing, icon, neutral surface, and semantic color system. Make the app accent freely customizable while preserving contrast. Keep app accent and physical keyboard LED color separate. Verify dark/light themes, keyboard accessibility, visible focus, contrast, reduced motion, and realistic window sizes.
